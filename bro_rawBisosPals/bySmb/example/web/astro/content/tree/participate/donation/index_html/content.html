The Libre-Halaal Foundation is a U.S. non-profit organization registered in the State of Washington (EIN: 91-2038354), and is tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Service regulations.

All contributions made to the LHF are tax deductible in accordance with these regulations. Any organization or individual wishing to support the goals of the LHF is requested to make an appropriate donation. Monetary contributions may be mailed to the LHF at: 

    Libre-Halaal Foundation
    3610 164th PL SE
    Bellevue, WA 98008

All donated funds will be used exclusively in support of the purposes and goals of the LHF, as defined in the Libre-Halaal Foundation Policies & Procedures.

The board of directors and officers of the LHF donate their time and services on a purely voluntary basis, and do not receive any financial compensation.

The Articles of Incorporation & the Bylaws of the Libre-Halaal Foundation are available upon request.
