** Vocabulary anchors

- =sys/= means /this specific running platform/ (self-reference), not
  a generic namespace.
- =sys/cbm/<realm>/<capabilityType>/<capability>/= is the two-level
  layering. Five realms (=platform=, =site=, =pals=, =user=,
  =collective=); four capability types (=hereWeb=, =hereMail=, =rpyc=,
  =induct=).
- Use =realm= not =band=.
- CBM sits on top of CBS via subprocess; it does not duplicate CBS
  content. =cbmProc.spcs= at the airflow leaf is a ~10-line pointer.

** Airflow leaf structure (canonical)

Three files at =sys/cbm/collective/hereWeb/airflow/=:

- =cbmProc.spcs= --- pointer via
  =cbmProc_seedInfo.setup(capabilityName="airflow", cbsSpec="airflow-cbs.pcs")=.
  The commented-alternative uses the absolute path form
  =/bisos/asc/web/bin/airflow-cbs.pcs=.
- =cbmProc.control= --- initial contents =available=. Written by
  =cbm_enable=/=cbm_disable=/=cbm_available=.
- =cbmProc.status= --- append-only log of skip/run/failure events.

** Walker at =collective/=

=sys/cbm/collective/ftoBranchProc.spcs= imports
=bisos.capability.cbmProc_seedInfo='s =leafProcessorNames()= and
=walkExamples()= functions and passes them to
=ftoBranch_seedInfo.setup(leafProcessors=..., leafExamples=...)=.
The walker then classifies =hereWeb/airflow/= as a Leaf definitionally
(via =cbmProc.spcs= match); other directories (=induct/=, =hereMail/=,
=rpyc/=, =rpyc/facterPerf/=) traversed as implicit auxBranches
(markerless walk per =bisos.fileObj= Deliverable 5).

* Key Design Decisions

** Realm/capabilityType layering

Directory shape enforced:
=sys/cbm/<realm>/<capabilityType>/<capabilityName>/=. Five realms
(=platform=, =site=, =pals=, =user=, =collective=), four capability
types today (=hereWeb=, =hereMail=, =rpyc=, =induct=). Not "band"
(that was a stale draft term).

Rationale: separates *ownership* (realm --- which BXO customizes
this) from *delivery mode* (capability type --- HTTP vs mail vs
service vs non-service).

** =here= renamed to =hereWeb= (with =hereMail= peer created)

Original =collective/here/= implied all-things-.here. Renaming
distinguishes HTTP-via-.here (=hereWeb=) from mail-via-.here
(=hereMail=). Renamed via =git mv= to preserve history. Empty
=hereMail/= created as sibling placeholder.

** CBM as pointer, not inliner (matches =bisos.capability= D4 pivot)

Original decision at planting time was to inline CBS content in
=cbmProc.spcs=. Pivoted before planting: =cbmProc.spcs= is now a
~10-line pointer with =capabilityName= + =cbsSpec=. CBM
subprocess-invokes the CBS at materialization time. The pre-existing
=airflow-cbs.pcs= stays put and unchanged.

** =bro_rawBisosPlatform/= symlinked to =/bisos/platform/= on raw-bisos

Editing this BRO's =bro_rawBisosPlatform/sys/cbm/= directly edits
what a raw-bisos host sees at =/bisos/platform/sys/cbm/=. Live
platform state.

** Container vs VM

The CBM framework layer (walker, gate, subprocess-dispatch, status
audit) all works in a container. The CBS's inner materialization
work (apt install, systemctl start, /etc/hosts writes, nginx config)
requires a VM host --- most CBS files include their own
in-container refusal gates (e.g. =airflow-sbom.pcs=). This is by
design; the CBM machinery is exercised in the container, and the
final VM leg of materialization is validated separately.

** Known limitation: g_csMain exit-code propagation

=bisos.b.cs.main.g_csMain= exits 0 even when a Cmnd raises. Result:
subprocess =rc= from a CBS may be 0 despite an inner failure, and
=cbmProc.status= may log =ran: cbs_materialize ok= incorrectly. This
is a =bisos.b= issue affecting many downstream consumers. Do NOT
work around locally here.
